Welcome to my website!

I am a second-year Computer Science PhD student at NYU Courant advised by [Benedikt Bünz](https://cs.nyu.edu/~bb/) and [Joseph Bonneau](https://jbonneau.com/). The focus of my research is on cryptography, mostly proof systems and accumulation.

Before graduating my PhD, I did a B.A./M.S. dual degree program in CS at Boston University, where I was lucky to learn cryptography from [Leo Reyzin](https://www.cs.bu.edu/~reyzin/) and worked as a TA for [Ran Canetti](https://www.bu.edu/cs/profiles/ran-canetti/)'s cryptography course.
